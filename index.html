Our Purpose

We exist to bring the diagnostic accuracy and quality standards you would expect from a city-level laboratory—right to your doorstep, making dependable healthcare accessible locally.

Director's Biography

Mr. Pradip S. Jadhav is the Founder and Principal Scientist of Smear Pathology, Indapur, with a strong academic foundation in Microbiology and Medical Laboratory Technology. With his scientific knowledge and hands-on understanding of diagnostic laboratory practices, he has built Smear Pathology with a clear focus on accuracy, reliability, timely reporting, and patient-centred diagnostic care.

Under his direction, the laboratory provides diagnostic services across key areas of pathology, including Histopathology, Biochemistry, Hematology, Clinical Pathology, and Microbiology. His broad understanding of these disciplines enables him to maintain a comprehensive approach to laboratory diagnostics, from routine investigations and blood analysis to specialized pathological and microbiological examinations.

Mr. Jadhav established Smear Pathology with a vision to make quality diagnostic services accessible to the people of Indapur and the surrounding region, reducing the need for patients and families to travel to Pune for reliable laboratory investigations. His approach combines scientific rigour with practical accessibility, ensuring that patients receive dependable reports within a reasonable turnaround time.

His commitment extends beyond laboratory testing. He places strong emphasis on quality testing, advanced diagnostic practices, professional standards, and responsible reporting, while continuously working to strengthen the laboratory's capabilities and services. The integration of modern laboratory technology with an experienced and dedicated team reflects his commitment to maintaining high standards throughout the diagnostic process.

Through Smear Pathology, Mr. Jadhav continues to work toward a simple but important objective: providing accurate diagnostic information that helps patients and healthcare professionals make informed decisions about health and treatment.
