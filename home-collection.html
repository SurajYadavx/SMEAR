Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an extra charge for home collection? +
A minimal home collection fee may apply depending on your exact location within Indapur. Please contact us via WhatsApp to confirm the exact charges.
How will I receive my reports? +
Once your samples are processed, we will securely send your highly accurate digital reports directly to your WhatsApp and email. You can also collect a physical copy from our laboratory if desired.
Are the home collection phlebotomists trained? +
Yes! All our phlebotomists are highly trained and qualified professionals who ensure a painless, safe, and completely hygienic sample collection process using single-use sterile equipment.
