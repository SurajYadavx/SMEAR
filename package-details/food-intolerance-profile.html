❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ's):

1. What does the Thyrocare Food Intolerance Profile include?
The Thyrocare Food Intolerance Profile is a detailed diagnostic test that screens for intolerance to 218 different food items across various categories such as dairy, cereals, meats, seafood, vegetables, fruits, spices, and nuts. This test detects IgG antibodies in response to specific foods, helping identify potential causes of delayed allergic reactions, digestive discomfort, skin issues, headaches, and chronic fatigue. All tests are conducted at Thyrocare’s fully automated, NABL and ISO-certified laboratories to ensure high accuracy and reliable outcomes.
2. Who should consider the Thyrocare Food Intolerance Profile?
This profile is highly recommended for individuals who frequently suffer from bloating, stomach discomfort, skin rashes, migraines, or unexplained fatigue. It is also useful for those who are following elimination diets or want to make informed decisions about which foods might be contributing to chronic symptoms. Thyrocare’s Food Intolerance Profile serves as a powerful preventive tool for improving overall wellness through dietary awareness and management.
3. Is fasting required before taking the Thyrocare Food Intolerance Profile test?
Fasting is not necessary for this test. You can schedule it at any time during the day, making it convenient and flexible. Since this test analyzes immune responses to foods rather than metabolic functions, eating before the test does not affect the results.
4. How is the sample collected and processed by Thyrocare?
Thyrocare provides free home sample collection across India. Once your booking is confirmed, a trained and certified phlebotomist will visit your location at the scheduled time to collect a blood sample. The sample is then safely transported to Thyrocare’s central diagnostic laboratory, where it undergoes analysis using fully automated systems. The entire process ensures high accuracy and minimal human error.
5. What is the cost of the Thyrocare Food Intolerance Profile?
The Thyrocare Food Intolerance Profile is available at a competitive price of Rs 6,999. This includes testing for 218 food allergens, free home sample collection, and a comprehensive digital report sent via email and SMS. It is an affordable solution for those seeking to understand the root cause of unexplained health symptoms linked to food sensitivity.
6. When will I receive my Thyrocare test report?
You will typically receive your test report within 72 hours after sample collection. Thyrocare ensures prompt digital delivery of your results, enabling timely consultation with a healthcare professional to interpret the findings and plan your dietary strategy.
7. Why choose Thyrocare for food intolerance testing?
Thyrocare is one of India’s most trusted names in preventive healthcare diagnostics, offering quality services backed by over two decades of expertise. With its vast network for home sample collection, fully automated testing systems, and adherence to NABL and ISO standards, Thyrocare ensures accurate, affordable, and accessible health solutions. The Food Intolerance Profile is a valuable tool for individuals aiming to personalize their nutrition and address chronic health concerns through informed dietary changes.
