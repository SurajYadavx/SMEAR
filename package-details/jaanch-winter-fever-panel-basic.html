❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ's):

Q: What is the Jaanch – Winter Fever Panel Basic?
Ans.
The Winter Fever Panel Basic is a diagnostic package curated by Thyrocare to help detect the underlying causes of seasonal illnesses during the winter months. It includes 37 essential parameters that help evaluate common symptoms like cold, cough, sore throat, fatigue, chills, headache, body aches, and fever. This test is especially useful during seasonal transitions when viral and mosquito-borne infections are more prevalent.
Q: Which tests are included in this package?
Ans.
The package includes tests for detecting serious infections like Dengue, Malaria, and Chikungunya, which are common during seasonal changes. It also covers essential routine health markers such as Complete Blood Count (CBC), Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (ESR), SGPT, and SGOT. These parameters provide insights into your immune response, liver health, and presence of inflammation or infection in the body.
Q: Why is this test recommended during the winter season?
Ans.
Sudden temperature changes during winter weaken the immune system, making the body more susceptible to viral and bacterial infections. Fever, cough, and cold are common but can sometimes point to more serious underlying infections. This test helps in early detection, ensuring prompt treatment and reducing the risk of complications.
Q: Who should take the Winter Fever Panel Basic?
Ans.
Anyone experiencing persistent cold symptoms, sore throat, body aches, or unexplained fever should consider this test. It is especially useful for individuals with low immunity, the elderly, or those living in areas where vector-borne diseases are common during the winter season.
Q: Is fasting required before the test?
Ans.
No, fasting is not required for the Winter Fever Panel Basic. You can eat and drink normally before sample collection.
