❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ's):

What is the Aarogyam XL Plus Package?
The Aarogyam XL Plus Package is a premium and most comprehensive health screening designed to assess overall health including metabolic health, immunity, hormonal balance, organ function and nutritional status.
Who should opt for the Aarogyam XL Plus Package?
This package is ideal for individuals looking for the most detailed full body checkup, those with lifestyle-related risks, hormonal concerns, exposure to environmental toxins, working professionals and anyone seeking complete preventive health monitoring.
What does the Aarogyam XL Plus Package include?
It includes Thyroid profile, Cardiac risk markers, Liver and Kidney function tests, Diabetes screening, Pancreatic enzymes, Vitamins, Electrolytes, Complete Blood Count, Immunity markers, Allergy tests, Inflammation markers, Autoimmune markers, Steroid hormones and Toxic Elements screening along with Complete Urine Analysis.
Why is this package important?
This package helps detect early signs of diabetes, heart risk, hormonal imbalance, autoimmune conditions, metabolic disorders, vitamin deficiencies, toxicity and organ dysfunction, enabling timely medical advice and preventive care.
Is fasting required for this package?
Fasting may be required for accurate blood sugar and lipid profile results.
How is the sample collected?
Samples are collected through home sample collection by trained technicians, ensuring a safe and convenient testing experience.
